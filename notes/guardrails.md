# Guardrails

## Things To Avoid

- Exposition-heavy AI explanations.
- Characters delivering ideological speeches instead of making pressured choices.
- Clean moral resolution.
- Overly neat ending.
- Turning Nico into a victim, symbol, or custody object without agency.
- Making Ethan a naive tech booster.
- Making Lena a simple anti-technology scold.
- Making Saul a villain because he understands power.
- Making Ruth a quaint outsider whose only function is observation.
- Treating AI companionship as obviously fake or obviously superior.
- Treating the singularity as a visible event with a universally accepted meaning.
- Letting courtroom procedure overtake the family story.
- Using news headlines as substitutes for lived consequences.
- Making Los Angeles a cartoon dystopia or Akron a sentimental refuge.
- Allowing the book's intelligence to outrun its emotional specificity.

## Writing Principles

- Conflict over explanation.
- Specificity over abstraction.
- Human stakes over technological detail.
- Material consequences over thematic labeling.
- Contradictory truths over balanced debate.
- Pressure-tested love over declared love.
- Embodied care over generalized empathy.
- Institutional language should reveal evasion, not become the novel's voice.
- Every AI system should have a practical function before it has thematic meaning.
- Every character's position should cost them something.
- The family should never become merely representative of society; society should be felt through what it does to the family.
- Let measurable improvement coexist with moral injury.
- Let real help coexist with dependency.
- Let privacy coexist with danger.
- Let endurance remain unresolved, active, and costly.

## Scene-Level Constraints For Future Drafting

- No scene should exist only to explain AI.
- No argument should end with one character clearly winning the thematic debate.
- Whenever a character says or implies a principle, the scene should pressure that principle through a concrete need.
- Legal, medical, technical, and policy details should enter through action, documents, decisions, obligations, or misunderstanding.
- Nico's interiority must not be reduced to what adults discover in logs.
- The AI companion should be represented through effects, choices, records, and consequences rather than seductive imitation of novelistic dialogue unless the drafting strategy later makes a deliberate formal choice.

## Ending Guardrails

- Do not end with AI rejected, accepted, or revealed as the single cause.
- Do not end with the family restored to the old structure.
- Do not end with Nico cured by either human love or technology.
- Do not end with Saul punished into moral clarity.
- Do not end with Ruth proven simply right about older ways.
- The ending should reframe need as something less economic, less instrumental, and more difficult: answerability, witness, and chosen obligation after usefulness has failed.
