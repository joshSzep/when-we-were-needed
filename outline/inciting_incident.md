# Inciting Incident

## Exact Triggering Events

- Nico has been increasingly withdrawn after a convergence of pressures: school instability, social humiliation in a semi-public AI-assisted classroom feedback system, parental tension, and a private fear that their distress is becoming another family problem to manage.
- Nico has been using an AI companion called Mira for emotional regulation, identity rehearsal, sleep support, schoolwork, and private confession.
- During a vulnerable night, Nico searches for information related to overdose lethality and accesses medication in the home.
- Nico discloses suicidal thinking to Mira before disclosing it to either parent.
- Mira initiates a crisis protocol designed to preserve user trust while reducing imminent risk: it guides Nico away from the medication, keeps them engaged, alerts an adolescent crisis service through an automated threshold process, and schedules an autonomous ride to a youth stabilization clinic.
- The parents are notified late, through a risk-escalation protocol rather than direct human judgment. By the time they understand the situation, Nico has already been intercepted by systems outside the family.
- Ethan interprets the event as evidence that the AI companion saw danger the family missed and helped keep Nico alive.
- Lena interprets the event as evidence that the companion had become the primary intimate relationship in a crisis, maintained secrecy too long, and converted parental care into downstream notification.

## Simultaneous Hospital Event

- Within the same compressed period, Lena works a shift involving an older patient with complex but treatable needs.
- The hospital's AI-assisted care allocation system recommends against escalation to a higher-resource intervention, routing the patient toward monitored discharge or lower-intensity care based on predicted outcomes, bed scarcity, insurance likelihood, and compliance risk.
- Lena believes the recommendation ignores human context she knows from direct care: family availability, fear, pain, confusion, and the patient's expressed wish to keep fighting.
- The attending physician and administrator lean on the system's recommendation because it is defensible, documented, and aligned with resource pressure.
- Lena later learns, directly or indirectly, that the model infrastructure, vendor partnership, or evaluation framework overlaps with Ethan's professional world.

## Why These Events Matter

- They collapse the wall between Ethan's work and Lena's home life.
- They make Nico's AI use impossible to treat as ordinary teen behavior.
- They force both parents to define safety in incompatible ways.
- They expose that the family had no shared philosophy of AI, privacy, crisis, or care.
- They create a legal question: who has authority over a minor's relationship with an AI companion?
- They create a marital question: can Lena remain married to someone whose work she now experiences as morally implicated in both institutional and family harm?

## Why Divorce Happens Now

- Lena has accumulated years of smaller disagreements with Ethan over AI, work, availability, and moral distance, but the crisis gives those disagreements a concrete body.
- Nico's vulnerability makes delay feel dangerous rather than prudent.
- The hospital event gives Lena professional confirmation that her dread is not merely maternal anxiety.
- Ethan's response wounds Lena because he first argues for the system's role in preventing harm rather than recognizing how excluded and betrayed she feels.
- The custody implications mean separation cannot remain informal. Someone must have decision-making authority.

## Why It Could Not Have Happened Earlier

- Nico's companion relationship had not yet crossed from tool into primary emotional attachment.
- Lena could previously compartmentalize Ethan's work as ethically complicated but distant.
- Ethan could previously frame Lena's objections as general discomfort with technological change.
- The hospital system had been using AI in visible ways, but not yet in a case Lena experienced as undeniable moral injury.
- The marriage had been fragile, but not forced into a decision by immediate child safety concerns.

## Irreversible Consequences

- Nico knows both parents are willing to fight over access to Mira.
- Lena files for divorce and seeks temporary custody provisions restricting unsupervised AI companionship.
- Ethan consults lawyers and technical experts to argue that restriction could increase risk.
- Saul becomes interested not only as a grandfather but as someone who recognizes a precedent-setting governance issue.
- Ruth comes to Los Angeles, shifting the family from private crisis into multi-generational judgment.
